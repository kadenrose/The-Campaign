About Mark Hampton

Mark Hampton is a longtime resident of Maricopa County, committed to making local government more responsive and more transparent. Mark prides himself as being an entrepreneur, husband, and father of five children. According to Mark, real change starts at the local level.

Mark has served on several community boards in the past where he focused on neighborhood development and safety. His contribution to the county's planning has brought much needed infrastructure improvements to the valley. Mark has a simple approach: listen more, talk less, and always put others first.

Mark was born and raised in Mesa, Arizona into a family of six. His childhood was spent volunteering at his church and joining student council at his schools. He graduated Scottsdale High in 1980 and the University of Arizona in 1984 with a bachelor’s degree in engineering.

Motivated by firsthand experience with outdated local policies, Mark left his business practice and is running for office in 2026.

Issues

Growth Management

Why it matters: Rapid growth is aging infrastructure faster than planned.

Mark's stance: Smart, long-term planning based on engineering experience.

What he'll do: Infrastructure audits, shorter development timelines, and better oversight.

Support for Local Businesses

Why it matters: Small businesses drive the local economy.

Mark's stance: Simplify and clarify business processes.

What he'll do: Streamline permitting and establish a business liaison office.

Public Safety & Emergency Planning

Why it matters: Safety must be proactive, not reactive.

Mark's stance: Prevention through modernized systems.

What he'll do: Improve training, funding, and inter-agency communication.

Community Development & Parks

Why it matters: Livable neighborhoods create strong communities.

Mark's stance: Equal access to safe public spaces.

What he'll do: Reinvest in older neighborhoods and expand shaded parks.
